## Verdict

Reaching 10 K with 50 mK per stage needs N = 200 thermally series stages. With COP = 0.5 per stage, the electrical power multiplier is prod(1+1/COP) = 10^95, so cooling 100 W would need about 10^97 W. That is not physically realisable. Each interface also drops R_c*q'' = 154.15 mK, which is 3.08x the per-stage lift, so a stage gives no net lift. Thermally parallel modules only multiply watts: 6,488 modules (0.00649 m^2) carry 100 W, but the temperature lift stays 50 mK.

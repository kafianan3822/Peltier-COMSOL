## Verdict

Reaching 10 K with 5 mK per stage needs N = 2000 thermally series stages. With COP = 0.2 per stage, the electrical power multiplier is prod(1+1/COP) = 10^1556, so cooling 100 W would need about 10^1558 W. That is not physically realisable. Each interface also drops R_c*q'' = 5.58 mK, which is 1.12x the per-stage lift, so a stage gives no net lift. Thermally parallel modules only multiply watts: 179,189 modules (0.179 m^2) carry 100 W, but the temperature lift stays 5 mK.
